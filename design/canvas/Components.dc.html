The five states
every screen renders all five — DoD gate
1 · Loading
Skeleton in the real layout — never a spinner over a blank page.
2 · Empty
Cart khali hai
Barcode scan karein ya koi cheez daalein.
3 · Error
Bill mehfooz nahin hua
Kuch bhi save nahin hua — cart waisa hi hai. Dobara koshish karein.
4 · Offline
Internet band hai
Koi farq nahin — bill banta rahega. Backup baad mein chala jayega.
5 · Permission
Sirf malik ke liye
Kharid ki qeemat aur nafa dekhne ki ijazat nahin hai.
Gate
A screen that cannot render all five does not merge. One test per state, enforced in CI.
